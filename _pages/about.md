I am a **first-year PhD candidate** at the [NLP Group](https://nlp.org.hk/) at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.ust.hk/en), supervised by Professor [Junxian He](https://people.dcs.ed.ac.uk/~junxhe/). My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing** and **Machine Learning**.

## Research Interests

My primary research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
