I am a first-year PhD candidate at [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/HKUST-NLP), advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://hx9175.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. degree from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with specific interests in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models **(VLM)
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
